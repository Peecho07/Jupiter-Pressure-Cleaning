## Problems today (checked October 15, 2026)

1. **No website.** The Google profile has 83 reviews at 5.0 and no website listed. Someone searching "roof cleaning Jupiter" has no page of his to land on. https://business.google.com/v/jupiter-pressure-cleaning/011258943377478726145/f44e/_
2. **Services menu is 4 lines with no detail.** Google lists only Power/pressure washing, Gutter cleaning, Roof cleaning and Soft wash cleaning, while reviews mention pavers, pool decks, shutters, skylights, fences and patio furniture. https://business.google.com/v/jupiter-pressure-cleaning/011258943377478726145/f44e/_/fullmenu/
3. **Photos have no captions or order.** The before and after shots customers praise sit unlabeled in the Google album. https://business.google.com/v/jupiter-pressure-cleaning/011258943377478726145/f44e/_/album/
4. **No owner replies to reviews.** https://business.google.com/v/jupiter-pressure-cleaning/011258943377478726145/f44e/_/rev/
5. **Booking is phone only.** No way to send a request with photos outside business hours.

## What the new design does and why

- **One page per service** so roofs, house washing, pavers and gutters each have a page Google can rank for "<service> in Jupiter, FL".
- **Quote form sends as a text.** He already works by text (reviews mention before and after photos by text). The form opens the customer's messages app with the request filled in so they can attach photos. No new inbox or software for him to learn.
- **His own words lead.** "On time every time" comes from his Google description ("WE SHOW UP ON TIME EVERYTIME!"). Coast Guard veteran ownership is in the trust bar.
- **Proof from real reviews only.** Seven short quotes copied exactly from Google, first names only.
- **Open now badge** reads his Google hours (Mon to Sat, 8 AM to 5 PM) in Eastern time.
- HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with hours, area, rating and services on Home and Get a quote.

## Brand notes

- There is no logo, website or brand color anywhere public. The wordmark (name set in Barlow Semi Condensed with a water drop mark) and the navy, Jupiter lighthouse red and aqua palette are proposals. Swap in his truck lettering or shirt colors if he has them.
- No prices are public, so every service says "quoted per job" instead of a "From $X". Ask him for starting prices; adding them would help conversions.
